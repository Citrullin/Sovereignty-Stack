## 0. Bypassing the Banking Well: deSWIFT & Sovereign IBANs

The legacy international banking system (SWIFT, SEPA) is the ultimate **Banking Well**. It exerts immense gravitational pull on capital, dictating who can transact, at what velocity, and under what conditions. It is an architecture of high mass, high friction, and complete measurement (Wavefunction Collapse) by central authorities.

To achieve **Metric Separation**, a Federated Manifold must bypass this well entirely without isolating its participants from the physical economy. 

### 0.1 The Sovereign IBAN (deIBAN)

Instead of forcing users to convert their fiat energy into volatile crypto assets (which subjects them to the tidal forces of centralized exchanges), the Sovereign Stack provides a **deIBAN**.

- **The UMI Anchor:** Users interact with a Unified Manifold Interface (UMI) which acts as a bridge. A local cooperative or geographic DAO provides an IBAN that is topologically linked to the user's Soulbound Agent.
- **Quantum Tunneling Fiat:** When a user receives fiat (e.g., a salary) to this IBAN, the Banking Well registers a standard ledger entry. However, the Sovereign Stack immediately mirrors this state as a **Transient State Packet** (e.g., a Fiat-backed stable state) within the localized manifold.
- **Velocity without Gravity:** The user can now route this value across the Sovereign Mesh at high velocity ($v_s$) without waiting for the 3-day legacy settlement cycle.

### 0.2 deSWIFT: High-Frequency Cross-Border Routing

When capital needs to cross geographic borders, SWIFT relies on correspondent banking—a slow, high-friction cascade of sequential state collapses. 

**The deSWIFT Alternative:**
- **Probability Routing:** Capital is not "sent" across borders. Instead, an intent to settle is broadcast to the Liquidity Mesh. 
- **Constructive Interference:** A localized Hub in the destination country (maintaining its own physical fiat reserves and Sovereign Utility) recognizes the intent and executes the physical payout locally. 
- **The Mesh Settlement:** The two localized manifolds settle the difference asynchronously via their internal Resonant Consensus, bypassing the Banking Well entirely. The capital effectively "tunnels" from one physical anchor to another, maintaining its superposition until the moment of local execution.
