## 15. Transient Harvesting & Systemic Recovery

In a collapsing manifold, significant energy remains trapped in Transient States—infrastructure, liquidity, and social capital that are still functional but have lost their long-term viability due to **Gravitational Failure**. We propose a protocol for **Systemic Harvesting**, where these "Ghost States" are utilized as fuel for the transition to a decoupled sovereign stack.

### 1. The Ghost State Phenomenon

A Ghost State is defined as a protocol or organization that possesses high capital density ($M_i$) but zero Sovereign Velocity ($v_s$). It is essentially a "Zombie Well" that continues to rotate within the legacy orbit.

- **The Opportunity:** Because these states are already decohering from their original "Sovereign Purpose," their resources (codebases, user bases, treasury reserves) can be harvested without disrupting a functional ecosystem.
- **Thermodynamic Recovery:** We treat these legacy assets as High-Entropy Fuel. By "liquidating" the social and financial commitments of the dying state (e.g., through recycling NFTs and Smart Containers), we release the energy required to achieve Escape Velocity.

### 2. Resource Packetization

Instead of a slow migration, we advocate for **Packetized Extraction**.

- **The Protocol:** Sovereign actors identify "Residual Value" within the legacy manifold. 
- **The Extraction:** This value is converted into **Transient State Packets** (tokens, ZK-proofs of reputation, or bifurcated code forks). These packets are then "tunneled" into the new Federated Manifold, leaving the hollowed-out "Ghost Shell" to be fully absorbed by the Banking Well.

### 3. Topological Recycling

Within QSP, we frame this harvesting not as a "Business Exit," but as Topological Recycling. To leave resources within the Legacy Black Hole is to contribute to its Singularity Density, further trapping others. Harvesting transient states is the only way to prevent the total loss of innovation energy, shifting the substance out before the facade collapses entirely.
