### 0.4. The Missed Path: The Web of Things DAO

The tragedy is that a viable path for **Metric Separation** existed. As early as 2022, the W3C WoT Community Group was presented with a vision for a **Web of Things DAO**, a self-sustaining, decoupled infrastructure for standard governance.

The vision was a direct response to the **technical fragility** of the W3C (persistent 500 Internal Server Errors effectively paralyzing global standards). The proposal sought to replace this decaying infrastructure with:
- **DAO-Managed Infrastructure:** A reliable Kubernetes cluster managed by the community.
- **Robust Substrate:** Hardware specs with a dedicated 4-Level Data Safety protocol.
- **Autonomous Compliance:** Using the DAO to manage licensing and verify standard compliance, ensuring the "Web of Things" remains an open ecosystem.

Berners-Lee’s distaste for this technology stems from institutional Ego-Mass. To acknowledge a DAO-managed web is to acknowledge a web that no longer needs a singular "Director", initiating a symmetry-breaking event that the W3C was unwilling to undergo.
