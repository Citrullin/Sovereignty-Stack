## 20. The Social Slag Ledger: Tracking Destructive Interference

In a highly fluid ecosystem, actors will inevitably fail, act maliciously, or generate friction. In QSP, this friction is termed **Social Slag**. It is the byproduct of Destructive Interference—actions that lower the overall Sovereign Utility ($U_s$) of the manifold.

### 20.1 Defining Social Slag

Social Slag is not a financial debt; it is a topological weight attached to a Soulbound Agent. It accumulates through:

- Repeated DSLA failures.
- Attempting to route malicious Transient State Packets (e.g., MEV extraction).
- Providing false telemetry to the AI Supervisor Swarm.

### 20.2 The Slag Archive and the Veto Dashboard

Because the system relies on TinyMeritRank ($C_r$) for governance and routing, it must explicitly track and surface bad actors without relying on subjective human witch-hunts.

- **The Objective Archive:** Every slashed DSLA and rejected malicious intent is cryptographically archived. This creates a mathematically verifiable "Slag Ledger."
- **The Veto Dashboard:** Hardware Operators and Tier 2/3 Board members have access to a Veto Dashboard that visualizes the accumulation of Social Slag across the manifold. 
- **The Quarantine Protocol:** If an agent's Social Slag exceeds their Resonant Coherence ($C_r$), the Outer Bridge API automatically throttles their bandwidth. Their probability of successfully routing a Transient State Packet drops toward zero. They are not "banned" by a central authority; they are topologically quarantined by the physics of the network.
