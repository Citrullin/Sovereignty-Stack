## 2. Tinyblock Governance: Resisting Spaghettification

In classical systems, governance is bought (Plutocracy), leading to rapid **Ego-Mass ($M_e$)** accumulation and **Identity Spaghettification**. Tinyblock implements a tiered, physically-anchored governance model to ensure the network topology remains fluid and resonant.

### 2.1 Tiered Access Control

Permissions within the Federated Manifold are gated by **TinyMeritRank ($C_r$)**, ensuring only those with high Constructive Interference can alter the system.

- **Tier 3 (The Board):** Requires a top 0.1% $C_r$. This 7-9 member Elected Board (80% elected via merit/stake, 20% public candidates) manages global Elastic Issuance and protocol forks.
- **Tier 2 (Maintainers):** Requires a top 5% $C_r$ alongside stable determination (e.g., a formal bonded stake). Grants write-access to the Federated Gitea and the ability to trigger **Actuator Oracles**.
- **Tier 1 (Sovereigns):** Open to anyone with a "Beep-verified" (NFC phase-locked) identity. Allows baseline participation and localized routing.

### 2.2 Particle Consensus: The Physical Check

Because Tinyblock operates as its own isolated, low-entropy **Sector Well** (not an L2 tethered to a central L1), it relies entirely on its own **Particle Consensus**—the physical hardware operators and Hub owners—as the ultimate check against capture by the Federated TradFi Banking Well.

- **The Physicality of the Veto:** If the Tier 3 Board attempts to capture the protocol (acting as a Banking Well), the Hardware Operators can refuse to sequence or route the manifold's state updates. The particles themselves decide if they accept a new particle into the network.
- **The Equilibrium:** The Board provides the Sovereign Velocity ($v_s$) and innovation. The Operators provide the Hardness (Mass) and physical security. If the Board overreaches, the Operators sever the Outer Membrane TOTP API. If the Operators restrict velocity, the Board utilizes Quantum Tunneling to migrate the state.

### 2.3 The Evidence-Based Appeal & Sovereign Court (EASC)

To prevent Governance Nihilism and the accumulation of **Social Slag** (false rejections), Tinyblock utilizes an escalating dispute system based strictly on on-chain data.

1. **The Prover Appeal (AI Re-Audit):** A 7-member committee of Soulbound AI Agents re-evaluates the telemetry using a different PPR seed-set to check for topological bias.
2. **The Sovereign Jury (Social Verification):** If contested, physical verification is required. Peers with high $C_r$ must physically attest to the industrial output via NFC Beep-to-Verify.
3. **The Particle Veto:** For permanent blacklisting, an Operator Veto (66% majority of high $C_r$ physical anchors) is required, utilizing the physical weight of the infrastructure to override the Board.

### 2.4 Vesting & Metric Shielding

Core contributors follow an 8-year backloaded vesting schedule (climaxing at 17.5% in Year 8). Clawbacks apply for Destructive Interference. This slow release prevents early contributors from accumulating enough Ego-Mass to jam the protocol before the local manifold reaches self-sustaining resonance.
