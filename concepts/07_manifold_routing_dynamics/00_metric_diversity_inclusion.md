## 0. Metric Diversity & Inclusion Latency

In classical networks (the "Builder’s Exchange"), we identify a critical failure point: the "Centralization of Attention." Sequencers and builders prioritize the most liquid structures, creating a tiered system where smaller, regional, or experimental manifolds suffer from high latency and market neglect. This leads directly to **Topological Jamming** and the accumulation of **Ego-Mass ($M_e$)**.

### 0.1 Measuring "Inclusion Latency" ($\Delta I$)

To maintain the Resonant Coherence ($C_r$) of the entire federated ecosystem, we introduce a continuous measurement: **Inclusion Latency ($\Delta I$)**. This is the time delta between when an intent (a Transient State Packet) is generated by a localized manifold and when it is formally routed through the mesh.

- **The Monoculture Flag:** If a Router’s throughput consists of >80% traffic from the largest institutional nodes, while smaller manifolds experience $\Delta I$ older than optimal thresholds, the Router's Diversity Score drops. It is flagged as succumbing to Institutional Gravity ($G_i$).

### 0.2 The Particle Squeeze (Anti-Monopoly Game Theory)

To prevent monopolies, the Sovereign Stack enforces a Nash Equilibrium where Routers are incentivized to maintain **Metric Diversity**.

1. **The Incentive (Constructive Interference):** Routers with high Diversity Scores receive a "Diversity Rebate" in the form of increased Thermodynamic Subsidy ($v_s$). It is mathematically more profitable to route "niche" physical utility than recursive financial loops.
2. **The Mechanical Squeeze:** If a Router is flagged for "Gravitational Favoritism", the broader network (via Particle Consensus) artificially deprioritizes that Router's bandwidth. To regain standing, the Router must prove they have cleared the backlog of the "Long-Tail" manifolds.
3. **The Result:** Innovation is no longer throttled by "On-Chain Shelf Space." A localized manifold for a small village in the Global South receives the same probability routing priority as a high-density capital node.

### 0.3 Preventing Soft Spaghettification

Institutions often use "Latency as a Weapon," soft-censoring competitors by making them too slow to be usable (a form of Spaghettification).

- **The Transparency Shield:** Because inclusion latency is a verifiable topological metric, any attempt to "slow-roll" a sovereign competitor is immediately visible to the AI Supervisor Swarm.
- **The Automatic Re-Route:** If a Router's Diversity Score hits a critical low, the Sovereign Smart Accounts automatically utilize **Quantum Tunneling** to re-route their Transient State Packets to "Diversity-First" paths, draining the monopoly’s momentum in real-time.
